# Ocean Ice Cream — native shop and six real servings

Ocean Ice Cream now has a dedicated pearl/turquoise fitted room, six-pan curved
refrigerated display, two stainless three-nozzle serving machines, controls,
levers, drip trays, storage, topping jars and retained native dining seats.
The front has its own physical cone signs and the rear has tiled fitout instead
of the shared food-room kitchen. All food/merchandise remains true geometry.

Six separately purchasable foods use the cat's existing private wallet:
strawberry cloud 45, blue lagoon 48, pistachio garden 52, mango sun 50,
vanilla pearl 40, cosmic rainbow 65. Prices are shown before the visit.
The new foods have independent stable IDs. The three preceding generic Ice
items retain their original prices and readable receipts/inventory; their
existing servings can still be consumed, but the shop offers the six new foods.

Automatic affordable selection rotates only after a durable Ice purchase,
with a stable cat-specific starting flavor. An explicit wish or player-selected
flavor takes priority. There is no additional AI request or new currency.

Native cats hold the actual articulated cone, lick and react, with a shrinking
portion during eating. Soft serve, two-scoop and rainbow forms use six bounded
immutable templates, waffle relief and colored toppings. Resting anatomy and
face reset through the native animator; reduced motion retains a stable grip
and disables licking. Owned-food personal use shares the same serving visuals.
One selected machine stream uses fixed six-instance buffers and uniform changes
only, with no continuous geometry/matrix uploads.

Verification evidence is recorded after the browser and release gates finish.
The local SwiftShader authoring/browser runs do not establish a Mac/iPhone
frame rate or photorealism. The full luxury Mall illustration remains an art
reference; this batch is not a claim of 85–90% visual similarity.
