# Phase A visual review — source01d0ae4, actual private native frames

The three frames exist; errors/httpErrors are empty. This is the architecture
prototype selected explicitly by the QA harness, NOT public default V7. The
root id/name are retained and tests validate actual floor/pool/stair openings.

Comparison to new concept sections A/B/D:

1. The pressure roof exists and is curved, but its low-opacity glass does not
   express the dome silhouette. Add true structural roof ribs, continuous
   cornices and a readable entry/front hierarchy. Do not claim a palace from
   the flat-looking floor photo; the original artifact remains unchanged.
2. The diagonal camera puts the expanded foreground too close and crops it.
   Front view also emphasizes a floor edge instead of a façade. Correct the
   framing/lens using a physically free viewpoint, without hiding terrain or
   removing walls. Top view shows the whole continuous plate and unchanged Mall.
3. Main entrance and façade composition are still absent. Build a real central
   portal aligned with the lower lift approach, supported columns/windows and
   gold/LED roof lines. Retain the physical pressure envelope and the moving
   shaft outside all upper floors. Re-capture before proceeding to furniture.

Furniture/materials/8-room menu and migration are NOT finished at this stage.
The prototype deliberately does not put another set of sofas onto old circular
platforms. It does not assert the full V7 functional/visual completion checklist.
