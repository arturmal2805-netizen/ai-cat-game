single active home. Manual and daily AI purchases for either housed spouse
cannot charge or create another. Existing duplicates move out of the world and
inventory into a permanent receipt archive; balances, historical spending,
purchase counters, original lot/model/owner and the surviving home's two seats
remain unchanged. Cached clients retain the archive, and simultaneous first
spouse purchases reject the second transaction atomically. Single-cat ownership
and the fourteen-day family introduction schedule are unchanged.
