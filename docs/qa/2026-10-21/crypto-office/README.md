# Crypto office and weekly personal investments

Only the existing job-crypto model is replaced: three open floors, reception
and waiting sofas, six native workplaces, continuous stairs, green/gold/cream
roof/sign, plants/lanterns and eight live candlestick displays. One fixed
instanced submission and a shared 65-price array serve all displays. Inspecting
the office hides its roof; leaving restores it. Original approved cat models
and the other five business models remain in use.

Official crypto employees independently consider trading every SEVEN game days.
A skip consumes that cycle; poor cats never request AI. One durable attempt is
reserved before a forecast, with per-world server coalescing and local fallback.
Fictional 45-second BTC/ETH paths, leverage <=3, fees, signed PNL and first-crossing
liquidation settle personal escrow once. Prices never make per-tick AI requests.
Saved trades, forecasts, history and next dates appear in Investments. Corrupt
escrow selects a backup; private wallets above ten million survive reload.
Family introductions remain fourteen days.

  wallet equals original balance plus final PNL, next attempt is seven days
  later. Desktop/phone Investments cards fit. Three complete reloads retain
  history, prices and balance, with one mocked forecast and no paid calls.
- Unit checks cover thirty simultaneous device calls/lost ACKs/provider restart,

  liquidation, 30 reloads, large balances and backup recovery. Expressions expire
