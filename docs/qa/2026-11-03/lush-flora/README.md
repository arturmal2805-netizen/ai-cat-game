# Layered tropical flora — checkpoint

The player approved the clearer water and requested substantially richer visible
vegetation across the retained eight water adventures. Reference runtime:
`e3fe835` (kept unchanged in a separate checkout). No economy, save, route, career,
animal/cat model, island coordinates or paid placement IDs are changed.

This draft increases ground beds from twelve to a maximum of twenty-four where
physical space permits; combines shrubs, broad leaves, flowers and ferns; fills
cliff tops and river margins; and anchors hanging vines/moss to the actual rock
surface. Large leaves use curved 28-triangle blades, shared opaque geometry and
native material batches. All plants are static. Full route/vehicle/walking
sweeps and real bank-cap positions determine planting clearance.

The physical water fix removes opaque-floor intersections through ripple peaks
and both wet channel edges. The approved water field, impacts and existing
single scenery-probe lookup remain. Basin beds have real shallow slopes and
shared granular sand. Ordinary and normalized barrel UVs retain their own metre
conversion. The source budget is fourteen shared charts: 2,130,408 download bytes,
10,136,244 mipmapped GPU bytes; coalesced selected-only decoding, three workers.

Shop images are rendered from these actual models at 960×640; other products
retain their released dimensions. Runtime still loads shipped lazy PNGs.

Final publication is pending the complete gallery, native UI/reload checks,
full suite/build and every-island camera audit. Software-renderer resource data
must not be represented as physical M2/iPhone FPS gains.
