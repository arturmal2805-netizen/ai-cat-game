# Eight water gardens and duplicate-vortex retirement

The photographed Crystal Vortex is removed from the shop, native model/routes,
previews and live placements. Its original 185,000-coin price is credited once
per proven owned copy. Jungle Coaster's existing 225,000-credit archive remains
independent. A capped wallet retains unpaid credit. Cached clients cannot buy
retired stock under an already credited receipt or resurrect it during a merge.
All other products, original reference cells, prices, build times, island
coordinates and approved animal/cat models are preserved.

The retained eight gardens use original shared wood/rock/moss/leaf/lawn surface
charts, opaque curved foliage, real cliff moss, dry plant roots and flowerboxes
clear of complete seat/vehicle/walking sweeps. The charts are decoded on demand
with three shared workers. Download: 1,884,229 bytes; shared mipmapped GPU storage:
8,738,142 bytes. The eight 256-square static scenery probes total 802,011 download
bytes / approximately 2.67 MiB mipmapped GPU storage. Their source revision is
validated; cube capture happens only offline. Gameplay retains the same two wave
samples and one environment lookup, native shadows and phone pixel ratio.

Receiving pools retain their exact shore contour with 3,100 ripple triangles,
24 fixed instanced underwater stones and the native caustic clock/water level.
Blue transmission/Fresnel, small waves and the placement-oriented probe retain
native impact buffers. Finite floors, walls and rails taper under the real basin;
rapids banks/foam stop at its shore. Closed rivers and halfpipes retain full paths.
