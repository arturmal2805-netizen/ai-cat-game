# Jungle Coaster retirement

The player explicitly requested complete removal of Jungle Coaster. This release removes its shop product, preview image, 3D recipe, route, landscape branches and ride reactions. The adventure collection contains nine products; the other 60 registered water attractions and all 19 islands retain their released records.

Legacy saves retire bag copies and placed/construction copies before ordinary normalization. Proven ownership receives the original fixed 225,000-coin purchase value per copy, once. The persistent retirement receipt retains unpaid credit at the 10,000,000-coin wallet limit. Cached clients cannot resurrect the object, omit the refund archive to collect again, or make a new cooperative purchase. Both local backup selection and server writes validate this archive.

Validation:

- `npm test`: 906 passed, zero failures, including eight retirement cases, purchase preservation, shared saves and the remaining rides.
- Node 22 production build and Worker dry-run passed. Approved animal assets remain unchanged.
- `retained-content.json`: all island records and remaining product descriptors match baseline `76026e9`, except the adventure collection's count label. Every seat of the nine retained routes matches the baseline at 1,001 samples, including timing and impact events.
- `gallery-report.json`: nine actual WebGL prefabs in normal, grazing, phone, basin/outlet and seated-rider views; stable water/animation buffers, zero static-submission pixel differences and no lost contexts. Browser timings are diagnostics, not physical-device FPS claims.

The native phone/SQLite audit additionally covers the real nine-card shop, withdrawal of both a bag copy and an unfinished placed copy, exactly 450,000 coins credited, preservation/replacement of the separate paid Mega Loops and two durable reloads. Its final result is recorded in `ownership.json`.

This targeted removal does not change the retained rides' materials, graphics, native renderer or performance policy. Publication evidence is recorded separately after both deployed sites are verified.
