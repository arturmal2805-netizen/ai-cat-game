The old isolated-model queue extended beyond its shore. The new entry and four
solo waiting points lie on the land. Nonsurface rides finish on a wide raised
promenade around the front of the receiving pool, then return to the entrance;
they no longer walk directly through the pool or beneath the descending flume.
Physical planks, handrails, piles/bracing and stone paths follow the actual route.
Closed rides settle smoothly instead of walking in place at an unchanged point.
The independent test checks route seams, land queues and dry/elevated returns.

All new scenery is prepared through the existing cooperative selected-view
builder and merged into static material batches. Vehicle rounds share one
immutable geometry. The old sixty water models, nineteen islands, save IDs,
prices, full phone DPR and all approved cat/wildlife assets stay unchanged.
The common-world camera/return gate is preserved in the preceding release's
QA folder. This original stylized artwork does not reproduce every painted
detail of the reference, and software-renderer checks are not device FPS claims.
