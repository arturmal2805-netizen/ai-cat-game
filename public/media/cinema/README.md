# Cinema shorts

Three original animated mini-stories starring SpongeBob, Gary, Patrick and
jellyfish. These are project-authored shorts, not copies of television episodes.
Character artwork, animation, captions, synthesized background music and sound
effects are generated offline by `scripts/render-cinema.py`. No external video
stream, downloaded soundtrack or player account is needed.

- `sponge-bubbles.mp4`: 18 seconds, a bubble surprise for Gary.
- `sponge-burger.mp4`: 20 seconds, sharing a towering burger with Patrick.
- `sponge-jellyfish.mp4`: 18 seconds, dancing with friendly jellyfish.

H.264 main profile, yuv420p, 960×540, 24 fps, AAC mono, MP4 fast start.
Only the current clip is loaded and decoded by the in-world cinema.
